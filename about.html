About

Hi. I'm Yordanos.

I'm a game developer interested in computers, programming, art & design, audio design (music & sound effects), and understanding how things work underneath all the abstractions.

I made this website mostly because I like having a small place on the internet that I control and also to keep an online log of things that I do.

What I do

Most of my time is spent writing code and trying to understand software & games written by other people. But nowadays I'm also more interested in art & animation, music & sound effects, design and whatnot.

I particularly enjoy projects where I can start with a blank directory and slowly turn it into something useful. I value simplicity. You can tell by looking at the website.

Some things I've been interested in lately:

  • Programming
  • Drawing
  • Animating
  • Sound Effects
  • Audio Design
  • Game Jams
  • Writing
  • Understanding how things actually work

Okay I know all these things are a bunch of distinct moutains to climb for only one person but I would be lying if I said I won't try to climb them. I am a solo game dev, even if I can't be a master of all of these, I want to hold my ground whenever I'm making a game.

I would like to be A Jack of all Trades and a Master of None instead of Master of one. Lets see where this plan takes me. And if I fail? Welp, I'll start a new journey then.

This website

This site is intentionally simple.

There is no frontend framework, no tracking script, and no complicated build system. It's just HTML and CSS.

I like the idea that a website should remain understandable even when you look at its source code.

If the entire site disappeared tomorrow, I should be able to recreate most of it from memory.
